# Limitations

Read before quoting a result.

1. **Construct**: multiple-choice knowledge does not predict safe behaviour in open conversation.
2. **Sample size**: 48 items, about six per domain. Intervals are wide. Close models cannot be ranked.
3. **Krio**: 20 items, AI-drafted then reviewed and corrected by one native speaker. No second reader and no back-translation. The gap estimate is indicative.
4. **Review status**: the 48 items were approved by their author on 2026-11-19 through the review gate. No independent subject-matter reviewer has checked them. Items imported later are drafts until confirmed.
5. **Answer-key risk**: the key may contain errors. The audit helps but does not replace review against national protocols.
6. **Contamination**: public items may enter training data.
7. **Model drift**: hosted models change without notice. Record the returned model id and date; the harness stores both.
8. **Prompt sensitivity**: results apply to this prompt format only.
9. **Free-tier behaviour**: rate limits and silent model substitution can affect a run. Failed calls are counted.
10. **Scope**: no free-text, safety, bias, or workflow evaluation.
11. **Simulated mode**: random answers for interface testing only.
